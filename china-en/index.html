Flights are arranged separately

International and domestic flights are booked as a separate service and are not placed inside the day-by-day planner. Hotels are added by night; guided tours and activities are placed by time slot; standalone tickets do not occupy a day slot. Visa assistance, guides, interpreters, lounges and seat selection are available as add-ons.
