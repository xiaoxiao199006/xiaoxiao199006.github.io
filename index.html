信息分散Scattered information

服务商、交通、住宿与体验资源难以统一了解与比较。Suppliers, transport, stays and experiences are hard to review in one place.

选择困难Hard to compare options

不同方案在路线、服务内容、预算与执行条件上差异明显。Routes, inclusions, budgets and conditions differ sharply between plans.

协调复杂Heavy coordination

多人、多城市、多供应商之间需要反复沟通与核对。Multiple people, cities and suppliers mean endless back-and-forth.

决策耗时Time-consuming decisions

搜集信息、比较选项、整理材料，往往占据大量时间。Researching, comparing and organizing materials takes days, not hours.

经纬易行的方式：以信息整合、方案规划与资源协调，把复杂的出行需求转化为清晰、可比较、可执行的解决方案，降低沟通成本与决策难度。 How we help: through information integration, structured planning and resource coordination, we turn complex needs into clear, comparable, executable solutions.

PLANNING FIRSTPLANNING FIRST

先把方案看清楚，再决定如何出发See the plan clearly before you decide to go.

根据您的出行目标、目的地、时间、人数、预算及特殊要求，我们整合相关信息、梳理可行路线、比较不同服务选项，并形成清晰的方案材料，帮助您做出更有依据的选择。方案规划是经纬易行的核心服务，而非附加项。 Based on your goals, destination, dates, group size, budget and special needs, we gather information, map viable routes, compare service options and shape them into a clear plan document — so your decisions are grounded, not guessed. Planning is our core service, not an add-on.
